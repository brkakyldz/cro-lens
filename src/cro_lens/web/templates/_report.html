How to read this report

  1. Read the summary above: who the page is for and its biggest problems.
  2. Start with the top tests. An A/B test shows half your visitors the current page and half a changed one, then keeps whichever converts better.
  3. Check each test's evidence (a screenshot and a quote from your page) and mark the finding Right or Wrong. Your answers train your next audits.
What the words mean
Conversion
The one thing the page wants a visitor to do, such as clicking “Get your free plan” or signing up.
ICE score
How we rank tests: the average of Impact (how big the win could be, 1–10), Confidence (how sure we are, 1–6; capped because we don't see your traffic data, and computed by code, not by the model) and Ease (how cheap it is to build, 1–10). Higher is better.
Check first
Every finding goes through two checks: code confirms its quote really is on your page, then a second model pass re-reads it. “Check first” means the quote is real but the second pass had doubts about the reasoning. It is not wrong; look before you spend on it.
Guardrail
A number that must not get worse while you test. More clicks are no win if the leads get worse.
LIFT
A common way to score a landing page on six questions a visitor asks: value, relevance, clarity, anxiety, distraction and urgency.
Persona
A simulated kind of visitor, such as a sceptical first-timer or someone comparing prices, used to see where different people get stuck.
